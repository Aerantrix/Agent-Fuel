## The headline: Agent Fuel against the two blunt controls

Step cap at 15 steps; account limit at 2 normal days, a day being 10 healthy tasks of the same model and prompt level. *Mechanisms only* is the controller without its run budget: the loop detector, the circuit breaker and the error classifier on evidence alone. Share of excess is the sum of savings above the minimum over the sum of excess across the refusal runs. Coverage counts refusal runs. Spoiled counts control runs the control halted, with the mechanism that did it; the comparison that matters is among controls that spoil none.
